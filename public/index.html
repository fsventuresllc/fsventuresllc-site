About

F&S Ventures LLC is a Delaware limited liability company based in Minnetonka, Minnesota. We design, build, and run a small portfolio of software products, each aimed at a specific, recurring problem a business runs into. We keep our products narrow on purpose: each one does one job, does it well, and is maintained for the long haul rather than left to rot after launch.
