About F&S Ventures

F&S Ventures LLC is a Delaware limited liability company based in Minnetonka, Minnesota. We design, build, and run a small portfolio of software products, each aimed at a specific, recurring problem a business runs into. We keep our products narrow on purpose.

Portfolio

The products we run today.

  • LedgerMedic

    Finds and fixes costly QuickBooks Online errors, such as stuck Undeposited Funds and customer payments counted twice.

    Visit ledgermedic.com

  • Sprinklerly

    Operations software for irrigation and sprinkler contractors: scheduling, dispatch, routes, work orders and backflow compliance.

    Visit sprinklerly.com

  • GrantClose

    Post-award grant tracking for small nonprofits: every reporting deadline with an owner, the evidence beside it, and a closeout packet at the end.

    Visit grantclose.com

  • GigDriverProfit

    Shows Android gig-delivery drivers the true profit per hour of each offer the moment it arrives.

    Visit gigdriverprofit.com

  • SPCC Plan Builder

    Tells small facilities whether the EPA's SPCC rule applies to them, then builds a self-certified Tier I plan with review reminders.

    Visit spccbuilder.com

  • Condo Records Portal

    An official-records portal for Florida condo associations, with the posting checklist and request deadlines the statute requires.

    Visit condorecordsportal.com
