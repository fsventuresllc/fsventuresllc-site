What we do

We find a narrow problem, build the tool for it, and keep running it.

No sprawling platforms. Each product is aimed at a specific, recurring problem a business runs into, from bookkeeping errors to compliance deadlines.

  • Pick one job

    We start from one recurring problem for one kind of business, and keep the product narrow on purpose.

  • Build it properly

    Each product is designed and built in-house, with its own website, its own privacy policy and terms, and a real company behind it.

  • Run it for the long haul

    Launch is the start, not the finish. We keep every product monitored, updated and supported after it ships.

How we work

The principles behind every product

  1. Plain English

    Every screen explains what it found and why, without jargon. If a rule or a regulation is involved, we show where it comes from.

  2. You stay in control

    Our products show you what they found and what they would change, and leave the final decision with you.

  3. Honest about limits

    Each product says clearly what it does not do, so nobody mistakes a tool for a lawyer or an engineer.

  4. Free to try

    Every product has a way to start without paying, so you can see it work on your own situation first.

The products we run today

Each one is live, has its own site, and is maintained by us.

  • LedgerMedic

    Finds and fixes costly QuickBooks Online errors, such as stuck Undeposited Funds and customer payments counted twice.

    Visit ledgermedic.com

  • Sprinklerly

    Operations software for irrigation and sprinkler contractors: scheduling, dispatch, routes, work orders and backflow compliance.

    Visit sprinklerly.com

  • GrantClose

    Post-award grant tracking for small nonprofits: every reporting deadline with an owner, the evidence beside it, and a closeout packet at the end.

    Visit grantclose.com

  • GigDriverProfit

    Shows Android gig-delivery drivers the true profit per hour of each offer the moment it arrives.

    Visit gigdriverprofit.com

  • SPCC Plan Builder

    Tells small facilities whether the EPA's SPCC rule applies to them, then builds a self-certified Tier I plan with review reminders.

    Visit spccbuilder.com

  • Condo Records Portal

    An official-records portal for Florida condo associations, with the posting checklist and request deadlines the statute requires.

    Visit condorecordsportal.com

  • Daycare Handbook and Contract Builder

    Builds a home daycare's parent handbook and enrollment contract from the provider's own answers, and lets parents read and sign them online.

    Visit homedaycarecontract.com

  • Studio Month-End
