Products

Three products, all live, all maintained.

LedgerMedic

LedgerMedic is software for keeping your books clean and your ledger honest — built for small businesses that want their bookkeeping to be accurate without hiring a full-time accountant to get there.

Visit ledgermedic.com

Sprinklerly

Sprinklerly is software for running and scheduling irrigation and sprinkler service work — built for the crews and small companies that install and service sprinkler systems and need an easier way to manage jobs, routes, and customers.

Visit sprinklerly.com

GrantClose

GrantClose is post-award grant tracking for small nonprofits — upload an award letter, confirm the reporting schedule it contains, give every deadline an owner, keep the evidence next to it, and export a closeout packet when the award ends.

Visit grantclose.com
