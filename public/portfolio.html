Products we build and run

Each product does one job for one kind of business. Every one listed here is live, has its own site, and is maintained by F&S Ventures.

  • LedgerMedic

    For small businesses, and the accountants who look after their QuickBooks Online books. Four mistakes quietly throw the books off: payments stuck in Undeposited Funds, Unapplied Cash Payment Income, deposits that count a payment twice, and credit card payments entered twice. LedgerMedic finds all four, explains each one in plain English, and fixes the first three at the source transaction after you preview the change.

    • Scans every QuickBooks Online company on your Intuit account
    • Read-only until you approve a fix, and every fix is previewed first
    • Free to connect and scan
    Visit ledgermedic.com

  • Sprinklerly

    For irrigation and sprinkler contractors who are running the business on paper routes, group texts and spreadsheets. Sprinklerly puts customers, service-area booking, route planning, dispatch, work orders and backflow-test compliance in one place built for the trade, so nothing falls through the cracks between the office and the truck.

    • Customers book into open arrival windows on the service days you plan
    • Route planning with real drive times between stops
    • Backflow tests and technician certifications tracked with the paperwork trail
    Visit sprinklerly.com

  • GrantClose

    For the executive director, development lead or program manager at a small nonprofit holding restricted grants. The reporting schedule usually lives in a PDF in someone's inbox. GrantClose reads the award letter, lets you confirm the schedule it contains, gives every deadline an owner with the evidence beside it, and exports a closeout packet when the award ends.

    • Finds the reports and dates in an award letter and shows the sentence each came from
    • Email reminders at 30, 14, 7, 1 and 0 days before each due date
    • Free for one active award, with every feature included
    Visit grantclose.com

  • GigDriverProfit

    For gig-delivery drivers on Android who want to know whether an offer is worth taking before they accept it. GigDriverProfit reads the offer notification the moment it lands, scores it by real net profit per hour after gas and wear, and hands you back to the platform's own app to decide.

    • Profit per hour counts fuel, wear and deadhead driving
    • Read-only: never asks for a platform login and never taps anything for you
    • Free plan available on Google Play
    Visit gigdriverprofit.com

  • SPCC Plan Builder

    For owners of small sites that store oil above ground. The federal SPCC rule (40 CFR Part 112) requires a written spill plan at many of them, and small facilities can often write and sign their own. SPCC Plan Builder works out whether the rule applies and whether you can self-certify as Tier I, assembles the plan from your answers, and reminds you when it is due for review.

    • A plain applicability verdict with the federal citation beside every answer
    • Secondary containment math with the working shown
    • Free to build and review on screen; you pay only for the clean export
    Visit spccbuilder.com

  • Condo Records Portal

    For Florida condominium boards managing 25 or more units, which since January 1, 2026 must post their official records on a website or app. Condo Records Portal gives owners the login-protected records section the statute describes, and keeps the 30-day posting clock and the 10-working-day records-request clock in one place.

    • A posting board for every record listed in Fla. Stat. 718.111(12)(g)2
    • A request desk that counts working days and sends reminders
    • Flags protected information, such as Social Security numbers, in uploads so you can redact before posting
    Visit condorecordsportal.com

  • Daycare Handbook and Contract Builder

    For licensed and registered home-based child care providers. Every family needs a handbook and a contract, and writing them from scratch or adapting a template takes days. The builder assembles both from your answers, shows which sections your state's licensing rules ask for in Texas, California and Florida, and sends each family a link to read and sign.

    • Sections and rule numbers mapped for Texas, California and Florida
    • Parents sign online, and you get a signature certificate for every document
    • Free to build and preview the whole handbook
    Visit homedaycarecontract.com

  • Studio Month-End

    For yoga, pilates and fitness studios, and the bookkeepers who close their books. Month-end means rebuilding sales, tax, tips, gift cards and refunds from a studio software export by hand. Studio Month-End maps your categories and payment types to your accounts once, then builds one balanced journal entry per month that you import into QuickBooks Online.

    • Your export is read in your browser and never uploaded
    • One balanced journal entry per studio per month, with a tie-out report
    • Free to map, preview and check your first month
    Visit studiomonthend.com
