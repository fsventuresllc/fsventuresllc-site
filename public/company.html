About F&S Ventures

F&S Ventures LLC is a Delaware limited liability company based in Minnetonka, Minnesota. We design, build, and run a small portfolio of software products, each aimed at a specific, recurring problem a business runs into. We keep our products narrow on purpose, and we keep running them after launch.

Founders

The two people behind every product

F&S Ventures is run by its two co-founders. One takes each product to launch; the other runs it from there.

  • Michael Schnapf

    Co-founder

    Michael has spent his career building and running software and e-commerce businesses, including as CEO of OpenSky and of MoreCommerce, and earlier in technology roles at Digital River. At F&S Ventures he takes each product from greenlight to launch: the spec, the build, quality checks and launch day.

  • Todd Frostad

    Co-founder

    Todd finds and screens the problems worth solving and runs every product after launch: marketing, growth and the customers who use it. He decides which ideas F&S Ventures builds next.

How we work

The principles behind every product

  1. Plain English

    Every screen explains what it found and why, without jargon. If a rule or a regulation is involved, we show where it comes from.

  2. You stay in control

    Our products show you what they found and what they would change, and leave the final decision with you.

  3. Honest about limits

    Each product says clearly what it does not do, so nobody mistakes a tool for a lawyer or an engineer.

  4. Free to try

    Every product has a way to start without paying, so you can see it work on your own situation first.
